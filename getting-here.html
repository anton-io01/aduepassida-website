Getting to Corato: address, access and parking

A Due Passi Da is at Via Duomo 81 — 70033 Corato (BA), Italy, in the historic centre. Agree your arrival time and access arrangements with us before travelling.

Arriving by car and parking

Private parking is available by reservation at the property’s stated rate of €20 per day. Confirm availability and ask for directions before travelling: the apartment address is not necessarily the parking entrance.

Ask us for a convenient meeting point to unload luggage. Always follow local road signs and any traffic restrictions in the historic centre.

From the station or Bari airport

Tell us your arrival station or airport and expected time. We can help with directions; a shuttle is available on request, with availability and cost to be agreed.

Stairs, luggage and check-in

There is no lift. Upper floors are accessible by stairs only. Check-in is arranged directly with us; mention any specific needs before arrival.
