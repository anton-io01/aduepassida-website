Two-bedroom apartment in Corato for up to 5 guests

A private 50 m² apartment at Via Duomo 81 in Corato’s historic centre. Two separate bedrooms and a living area accommodate families and small groups of up to 5 guests.

Sleeping arrangements

  • Main bedroom: a double bed for two.
  • Second room: a single bed that converts into a double, for one or two guests.
  • Living room: a single sofa bed.

Travelling with a baby?

A baby cot is available on request. Mention it in your message along with your guest count so we can agree the sleeping arrangements before arrival.

Space and comfort

The apartment has a private bathroom, air conditioning, Wi-Fi and a terrace. Ask about availability and conditions for kitchen use and additional services when making your enquiry.

Before you choose

Upper floors are accessible by stairs only; there is no lift. If you are bringing a pushchair or bulky luggage, contact us to plan your arrival.
