Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

1. Data Controller

The data controller for personal data is the manager of the B&B "A Due Passi Da", located at Via Duomo, 81 – 70033 Corato (BA).

Email: antoniomodugno01@gmail.com

2. Types of Data Collected

The website collects the following personal data:

  • Contact data: name, surname, email, phone number (optional)
  • Booking data: arrival and departure dates, number of guests, room type, special requests
  • Technical data: technical cookies necessary for website functioning (localStorage for cookie consent)

3. Purpose of Processing

Data collected through the quote request form is processed for the following purposes:

  • Respond to quote requests
  • Manage property bookings
  • Communicate with the client to organize the stay

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Data processing is based on the explicit consent provided by the user when submitting the form (art. 6, par. 1, lett. a of GDPR).

5. Data Retention

Personal data is retained for the time necessary to manage quote requests and any bookings. In case of no booking, data is deleted within 12 months of collection.

6. Third-Party Communication

Data collected through the form is sent and managed via the Formspree service (https://formspree.io), a GDPR-compliant external provider. No other communications to third parties are made, except as required by law.

7. Cookies

The website uses exclusively technical cookies necessary for the functioning of the consent banner (localStorage). No profiling or tracking cookies are used.

8. Data Subject Rights

The user has the right at any time to:

  • Access their personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request data deletion
  • Object to processing
  • Request limitation of processing
  • Request data portability

To exercise your rights, contact: antoniomodugno01@gmail.com

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The last update date is indicated at the top of the page.
