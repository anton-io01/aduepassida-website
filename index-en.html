The Apartment

A private apartment in the heart of Corato

A Due Passi Da is a 50 m² private apartment at Via Duomo 81 in the historic center of Corato (BA). With a double bedroom, a single bedroom and a sofa bed in the living room, it accommodates up to 4–5 guests. It is ideal for families and small groups who want to experience Puglia with the comfort of a private home.

  • 50 m² · 2 bedrooms · up to 4–5 guests
  • Private apartment with its own entrance
  • Soundproofing · City view
  • Location rating 9.5/10
  • 700 m from Corato train station
Upper floors are accessible only by stairs. There is no elevator.
