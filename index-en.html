The Structure

A private apartment of 50 m² in the heart of the historic center

A Due Passi Da is not a traditional B&B, but a private apartment located on the first floor of a historic building in the center of Corato. Perfect for families and couples who want an authentic experience in Puglia.

The apartment consists of 2 bedrooms and can accommodate up to 4-5 guests, making it ideal for families with children or groups of friends.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your stay

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is flexible upon agreement. Check-out is by 11:00 AM. We recommend communicating your arrival time in advance.

Is breakfast included?

Yes, breakfast is included in the rate. You will find basic items for a continental breakfast in the equipped kitchen.

Is parking available?

The apartment is in the historic center where traffic is limited. There are paid parking lots about 300-400 meters away.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are not allowed. Please contact us directly for special needs or exceptions.

Is the apartment suitable for families with children?

Yes, the apartment is perfect for families. With 2 bedrooms it can accommodate up to 5 people comfortably.

Is Wi-Fi available?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available throughout the apartment.
